My Ruhie,

Before the world wakes and long after it quiets down, you're still the thought my mind keeps returning to. Not loud, not much — just quietly, steadily true, the way morning light finds its way in anyway.

Today the year turns for you again. And with it comes a wish I've been carrying for a while now: that you keep laughing the way you do, that your patience keeps softening the hardest days, and that you never stop chasing the things that make your eyes light up.

You don't have to try to be anyone's favourite part of the day. You just are. So here's to you — every year a little more you, every doubt a little smaller, every dream a little closer.

Happy birthday, Ruhie. I'm glad the world has you in it. I'm gladder still that some of my days get to have you in them too.

— your Abdullah
